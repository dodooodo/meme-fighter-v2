# Pink Star — M9P Art Pack Requirements

- `BASE_FIGHTER`: normal Pink 250-frame body pack.
- `MODE_FIGHTER`: `true_face`, complete alternate moveset body pack.
- `PROJECTILE`: `sonic_l1`, `sonic_l2`, `sonic_l3`.
- `WORLD_EFFECT`: `sonic_charge`, `sonic_release`.
- TRUE_FACE effects: `true_scream_l1`, `true_scream_l2`, `true_scream_l3`.
- `ULTIMATE_SCREEN`: `true_face_background`.

Normal Pink special body frames only show inhale/charge/release/recoil. The sonic wave's travel animation is a detached projectile. TRUE_FACE does not belong in the tail of normal Sheet 10; normal Ultimate Sheet 10 ends at transformation handoff, after which Presentation uses the `true_face` mode pack when gameplay mode becomes authoritative.
